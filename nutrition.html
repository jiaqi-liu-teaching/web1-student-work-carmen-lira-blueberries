Colorful, healthy and delicious!
Blueberries are often called a “superfood”, and are among the most nutrient-dense berries. This small but mighty berry is loaded with vitamins, minerals, dietary fiber, and antioxidants. They help lower blood pressure, improve memory, aid in exercise recovery, and more.

So, grab a boost of mighty blue! It is a smart, delicious and easy choice you can make every day.
Health benefits of blueberries
Low in calories.
High in nutrients.
One cup contains:
13% of Fiber (Daily Value)
14% of Vitamin C (DV)
24% of Vitamin K (DV)
85% Water
84 calories
21.5 grams of carbohydrates
Antioxidants.
Antioxidants protect your body from free radicals that damage cells and contribute to aging and diseases.

Blueberries have one of the highest antioxidant levels of all common fruits and vegetables.
Lower blood pressure.
Eating blueberries every day for a month can significantly improve blood flow and blood vessel dilation, reducing high blood pressure (a major risk factor for heart disease).
Improve memory and maintain brain function.
A 2023 study found that consuming one cup of fresh blueberries each day can help maintain brain function and improve memory in older adults.
Manage blood sugar.
Research suggests that anthocyanins in blueberries have beneficial effects on insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism, which lower the risk of metabolic syndrome and type 2 diabetes.
Reduce muscle damage after exercise.
In a 2018 study, blueberries were found to improve exercise performance and decrease certain inflammatory markers minimizing soreness, which could aid in recovery.
