Three Easy Blueberry Recipes
When you are making recipes with beautiful fresh blueberries, you'll want to keep a few things in mind. Fresh blueberries are at their best from May until early October.

Look for firm, deeply colored berries that are uniform in size. Blueberries will stay fresh for about 7 days. Before extra berries go bad, you can freeze them. To freeze blueberries, arrange them on a baking sheet in a single layer to remove external moisture for a couple of hours. Then put them in a plastic bag or other container. They should last in the freezer until blueberry season next year!

Blueberry Pancakes
Fluffy pancakes with ground flax seed and blueberries for a healthier, fiber-filled pancake.

Makes 4 Servings
INGREDIENTS
1½ cups dry pancake mix
½ cup flax seed meal
1 cup skim milk
2 large eggs
1 cup fresh blueberries
Prep Time: 5 mins
Cook Time: 10 mins
Total Time: 15 mins
DIRECTIONS
- Place a large nonstick skillet over medium heat.
- Stir together pancake mix and flax seed meal in a large bowl.
- Whisk together milk and eggs in a measuring cup until well combined; pour into pancake mix mixture and stir until batter is just moistened.
- Spoon ¼ cupsful batter onto the hot skillet. Sprinkle with as many blueberries as desired.
- Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip and cook until browned on the other side.
- Repeat with remaining batter and blueberries.
